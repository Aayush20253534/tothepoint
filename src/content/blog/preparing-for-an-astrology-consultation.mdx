## Start with your questions

A useful astrology consultation begins with a clear question. Write down the subject you want to discuss and the context that matters to you. If you have several concerns, put them in order so you can agree on the scope before the session.

For example, a career conversation could focus on a change of role or uncertainty about your next step. A relationship discussion could focus on compatibility or a question about partnership. A practical question is easier to discuss than a request to predict every part of the future.

Browse the [consultation services](/services) before enquiring. Kundli analysis, numerology, Vastu and Muhurat use different starting information. You do not need to select several services simply because you have more than one question; explain your priorities first.

## Prepare relevant details

For [Kundli analysis](/services/kundli-analysis), have your birth date, recorded birth time and birthplace available. Write the town or city and country clearly. If the time is approximate or unknown, state that directly. Do not present an estimated time as a confirmed record.

For [marriage and relationship guidance](/services/marriage-relationship), ask what information is needed and obtain the other person's permission before sharing their details. A compatibility reading should support a conversation, not replace mutual consent, communication or your own judgment.

A [Vastu consultation](/services/vastu) may involve a floor plan or questions about the use of a space. Ask which details are relevant before sending documents. For [Muhurat guidance](/services/muhurat), note the event location, possible dates and practical constraints. Keep building safety and event logistics with the appropriate professionals.

## Agree on the session

Before confirming an appointment, clarify:

- Which topic or topics the session will cover.
- The consultation language, format and expected duration.
- The fee and payment arrangements.
- Whether written notes, reports or follow-up discussions are included.
- What happens if either person needs to reschedule.

Ask for clarification if a detail is unclear. Sending a website enquiry or opening a WhatsApp chat does not automatically reserve a slot. Only share the information needed for the agreed service, and avoid sending identity documents or unrelated personal records.

## Visiting or connecting online

For a [Prayagraj office consultation](/astrologer-in-prayagraj), confirm the exact address, directions and time before travelling. Prayagraj is also known as Allahabad; both names refer to the same city.

For an [online consultation](/online-astrology-consultation), give your current city and country and confirm the appointment in your local time zone. Choose Hindi or English when enquiring and agree on the call or video format in advance. The [international scheduling guide](/blog/online-astrology-abroad-time-zones) explains how to keep birth information and appointment details separate.

During the discussion, ask what an interpretation means and take notes if useful. Astrology is an interpretive tradition rather than a guarantee. Use the conversation as one perspective alongside practical information, your own decisions and appropriate professional advice.
